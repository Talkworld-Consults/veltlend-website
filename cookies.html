Cookie Policy

How VeltLend approaches cookies and similar technologies on the prelaunch website.

Last updated: 15 August 2026VeltLend

1. What cookies are

Cookies are small files or similar technologies that websites may store on a device or use to remember information about a visit. They can support functionality, preferences, security and measurement.

2. VeltLend prelaunch website

The current VeltLend landing page is intended to function primarily as a static informational and waitlist website. We aim to keep cookies and similar technologies limited to what is reasonably necessary for operation, security and any measurement that we intentionally enable.

3. Essential technologies

Some technologies may be necessary for the website to work, protect against abuse or support basic functionality. Where a technology is strictly necessary, it may not be treated in the same way as optional analytics or marketing technologies under applicable rules.

4. Analytics and optional technologies

If VeltLend introduces analytics, advertising pixels or other optional tracking technologies, we will update this policy and, where required, provide an appropriate choice mechanism before using technologies that require consent.

5. Browser controls

You can control or delete cookies through your browser settings. Disabling certain technologies may affect site functionality.

6. Updates

This policy may be updated when the technologies used on the website change. The updated date at the top of the page will indicate the latest revision.
